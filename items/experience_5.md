# Turning cardboard into an idea

## What was it that you did?

I helped develop StuDock, a desk organizer made from recycled cardboard for our Group Maker Project. I proposed the name and original organizer idea, shared sketches with the group, built and trimmed the prototype, measured its parts, and helped format our presentation.

## When did you do it?

October–November 2026, during the project briefing and prototype development stages.

## What was good about it?

The project let me connect design ideas to a physical object. We considered how shallow compartments, removable dividers, large labels, and contrasting colours might make supplies easier to find and reach. It helped me practise considering users while working with everyday materials.

## What was your favourite memory of it?

A highlight was turning the original sketch into a cardboard prototype that we could use for usability testing. Having an object to handle made the idea much easier to discuss with my group.

## What didn’t you enjoy about it?

Measuring, cutting, and adjusting cardboard required patience. A detail that looked simple in a sketch could take several changes to work in the prototype. Coordinating the product and presentation also took consistent effort.

## Give your experience a letter grade

B — For the most part enjoyable. I liked developing something useful, even though the small adjustments could be time-consuming.

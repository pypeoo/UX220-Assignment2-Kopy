# Being creative in Design

## What was it that you did?

I contributed to the Art Hawks council through marketing and creative work, including designs for social media. I put time into making posts that represented our activities and helped students notice what we were doing.

## When did you do it?

During my first year at Laurier, through my involvement with the Art Hawks council.

## What was good about it?

It gave me a reason to keep creating outside of class. I could practise visual communication for a real student audience and think about how colour, layout, and wording work together. That experience connects directly to my interest in graphic design and UX.

## What was your favourite memory of it?

The work I created for our posts is what I remember most. Those pieces hold memories of my time on the council, which is why preserving them matters to me.

## What didn’t you enjoy about it?

It can be difficult when the effort behind a design is less visible than the final image. I learned that creative work also needs clear communication about its purpose and the time it takes.

## Give your experience a letter grade

B — For the most part enjoyable. I valued the creative opportunities and the memories connected to my work.

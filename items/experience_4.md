# A little room for music

## What was it that you did?

I spent time playing piano and guitar on campus. Keeping music in my routine meant returning to practise, even when studying and other responsibilities made my schedule busy.

## When did you do it?

During my time at Laurier, including practice sessions in Room 004 of the Research and Academic Centre.

## What was good about it?

Music gave me a creative outlet alongside design and schoolwork. It also offered a way to share a part of my life with my family in Vietnam. Making time for something I enjoy supports my goal of having a balanced life with strong family connections.

## What was your favourite memory of it?

My favourite memory is recording videos of myself playing and sending them to my parents in Vietnam. I also love how the room’s acoustics make playing and singing feel special.

## What didn’t you enjoy about it?

Practising in a shared campus space can feel awkward when someone walks in. Finding time to practise consistently also takes planning around classes and work.

## Give your experience a letter grade

A — Couldn’t be better. Being able to enjoy music and share it with my parents makes this experience especially meaningful to me.

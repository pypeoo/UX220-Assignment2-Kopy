# Welcoming the next chapter

## What was it that you did?

I worked as a Brantford Recruitment Ambassador, helping welcome prospective students and families. I prepared campus tour information, practised my delivery, and used personal stories to explain student life. I also participated in recruitment events.

## When did you do it?

Fall 2026, including tour preparation and recruitment events in September and October.

## What was good about it?

The role pushed me to practise English and become more comfortable speaking to new people. It also reminded me to consider what visitors need to know, rather than only what I want to say. Listening and explaining clearly are skills I can carry into UX research and future work.

## What was your favourite memory of it?

A memorable part of preparing my tour was bringing in my own stories about campus, including playing music in the Research and Academic Centre. Sharing something personal helped the tour feel more like a conversation.

## What didn’t you enjoy about it?

I found it challenging to remember every required detail while speaking confidently. Feedback on my reverse tour showed me that I needed to project my voice and describe some services more clearly. Improving takes repeated practice.

## Give your experience a letter grade

B — For the most part enjoyable. I enjoy connecting with visitors, and I am still building confidence in the role.

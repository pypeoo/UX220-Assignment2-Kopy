# As a freshman, I've:

## What was it that you did?

I worked as a Vice President of Marketing for the Art Hawks Council of FYLP in my first year. My role was to design promotional materials, manage social media, and promote on-campus events for incoming and current freshmen, helping them feel involved.

## When did you do it?

During my first year at Wilfrid Laurier University (from Oct 26 to May 26), in Grand River Hall.

## What was good about it?

The council gave me opportunities to meet people and contribute through my interest in design. It helped me practise turning an idea into something other students could understand and connect with. It's not just about a better network with positive relationships, but also stronger abilities, including teamwork, communication, and leadership.

## What was your favourite memory of it?

I met many people, from students to academic advisors. I got to show off my skills, especially by bringing them into real life with real people. We got free food and prizes after we finished too; to be honest, that was the best part:)

## What didn’t you enjoy about it?

As a freshman, even harder as an international. I was overwhelmed with on-campus tasks while working on assignments and midterms. However, I’ve learned that it’s fine to bring up the problems so we can resolve them together. I evaluated the people around me differently, positively.

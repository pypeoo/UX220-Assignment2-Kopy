My experiences

01

FYLP · Leadership

My first year at Laurier

Getting involved in FYLP

What was it that you did?

I worked as a Vice President of Marketing for the Art Hawks Council of FYLP in my first year. My role was to design promotional materials, manage social media, and promote on-campus events for incoming and current freshmen, helping them feel involved.

When did you do it?

During my first year at Wilfrid Laurier University, while I lived in Grand River Hall.

What was good about it?

I met people outside my classes and felt more connected to campus. I received a lot of benefits, not just a better network with positive relationships, but also stronger abilities, including teamwork,

What was your favourite memory of it?

Receiving recognition for my involvement was a really nice moment. It made me feel that my time and effort mattered and reminded me how much I had gained by getting involved.

What didn’t you enjoy about it?

Balancing council responsibilities with coursework could be stressful when deadlines came together. I had to learn how to plan my time and be realistic about how much I could take on.

Give your experience a letter grade.

B — For the most part enjoyable. It took effort, but the connections and experience made it worthwhile.

02

Art Hawks · Marketing

September 2026–May 2027

Marketing for Art Hawks

What was it that you did?

I volunteered as Vice President of Marketing for the Art Hawks Council within FYLP. I designed promotional materials, managed social media, and promoted campus events for incoming and current first-year students. Basically, I encouraged people to join in!

When did you do it?

Sep 26 to May 27, first year at Laurier.

What was good about it?

I built my networking, communication, time management, and design skills. As a UX student, that experience was valuable.

What was your favourite memory of it?

I loved meeting different people, from students to academic advisors, and seeing my designs used for actual events. We also got free food and prizes after finishing activities.(Tbh, that's the best part.:)

What didn’t you enjoy about it?

As an international student, I sometimes felt overwhelmed balancing council tasks with assignments and midterms. Still, I learned to speak up when I needed help so we could work through things together.

Give your experience a letter grade.

B — For the most part enjoyable. It got stressful sometimes, but the people made it worth the effort.

04

Piano & Music

During my time at Laurier

A little time for piano

What was it that you did?

I spent time playing piano and guitar at the Research and Academic Centre (RAC) on campus. Making music part of my routine helped me relax and stay creative.

When did you do it?

During my time at Laurier, I practised in Room 004 whenever I had some free time.

What was good about it?

Music gave me a break from assignments and a chance to do something I enjoyed. It also gave me something to share with my parents in Vietnam, helping us stay connected even from far away.

What was your favourite memory of it?

My favourite part was recording videos of myself playing and sending them to my parents. Their compliments always made my day and motivated me to keep practising.

What didn’t you enjoy about it?

I felt a little awkward when someone walked in while I was practising. Having an unexpected audience made me nervous. :(

Give your experience a letter grade.

A — Couldn’t be better. Playing music and sharing it with my parents made these moments special to me!
